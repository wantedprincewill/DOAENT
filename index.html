FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast can you deliver?

Flash is delivered in 3–5 working days, Strobe in 2–3 and Prism within 48 hours. Add Rush delivery to get any design in 24 hours.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. Plans run month to month. Tell us before your next billing date and we’ll finish anything already in progress.

What files do I get?

Print-ready PDF plus high-resolution PNG and JPG, sized for every platform you post on. Motion and video come as MP4. Source files are available on request.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work remotely with artists, labels and promoters anywhere and quote international projects in USD.

What if I need something that isn’t listed?

Tell us what you have in mind. Festival identities, stage visuals and full campaign rollouts are quoted per project.
