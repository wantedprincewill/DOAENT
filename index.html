The studio

From the flyer to the feature film

DOA ENT is the entertainment arm of Doa Designz: the same design discipline and quality control, tuned to the pace and visual language of rave, club and music culture. We’re growing in deliberate phases.

  1. Phase 01 Now

    Visual design for events & artists

    Still graphics, video animation and editing for rave and music promoters, DJs and artists.

  2. Phase 02

    Artist & event branding at scale

    End-to-end identity systems for artists, labels and festivals: merch, stage and venue branding, campaign rollouts.

  3. Phase 03

    Full video production

    Shoot-and-edit music videos, event recap films, artist documentaries and EPKs.

  4. Phase 04

    Movie & long-form

    Short films and features, taking our visual storytelling into long-form entertainment.

  • Design-led. Every deliverable grounded in strong visual fundamentals, not just fast turnaround.
  • Culture-fluent. Built with genuine familiarity with rave, club and music-scene aesthetics.
  • Scalable. A service structure designed to grow from graphics into full production and film.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What’s your typical turnaround time?

It depends on the package: 5–7 business days on Flash, 3–5 on Strobe and 48 hours on Prism. Add Rush Delivery to any deliverable for a guaranteed 24-hour turnaround.

Can I cancel my monthly plan?

Yes. Monthly plans roll month to month. Let us know before your next cycle starts and we’ll wrap up any deliverables already in progress.

What file formats do I receive?

Print-ready PDFs and high-resolution PNG/JPG for every piece, sized for each social platform you need. Motion work ships as MP4, with source files available on request.

Do you work with artists outside Nigeria?

Absolutely. We’re based in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt and work remotely with artists, labels and promoters wherever they are.

What if I need something not listed here?

Tell us about it. Custom scopes like full festival identities, stage visuals or campaign rollouts are quoted individually. Use the form below.
